About BSides Delhi

What is BSides?

Security BSides is a global series of community-organised events. Each event is independently run by local volunteers. The idea is simple: create an open platform where security researchers, students, professionals, and hobbyists can present ideas and talk to each other without a corporate filter.

BSides Delhi

BSides Delhi brings that format to the National Capital Region. We have been gathering the Delhi security community for talks, hands-on workshops, a Capture The Flag contest, and themed villages.

Code of Conduct

BSides Delhi is dedicated to a harassment-free experience for everyone. We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form. Anyone violating these rules may be sanctioned or expelled from the event, without a refund, at the discretion of the organisers.

Report issues to any organiser or mail poc@bsidesdelhi.com.
