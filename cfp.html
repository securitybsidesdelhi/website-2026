Call for Papers

CFP STATUS: OPEN - ROLLING REVIEW UNTIL SLOTS FILL

We want original security content. First-time speakers are welcome and encouraged. If you are not sure your idea is good enough, submit it anyway and let us decide.

Tracks

  • Offensive - exploitation, red team, tooling, hardware
  • Defensive - blue team, detection, DFIR, hardening
  • AppSec - web, mobile, API, cloud, supply chain
  • Research - reversing, crypto, protocol analysis
  • Human - policy, privacy, awareness, career

Formats

TALK ........... 40 minutes + 5 min Q&A
SHORT TALK ..... 20 minutes
WORKSHOP ...... 90 to 180 minutes, hands-on, bring-your-laptop

How To Submit

Send the following to poc@bsidesdelhi.com with the subject line CFP Submission:

  • Talk title
  • Abstract (200 to 400 words)
  • Format (talk / short talk / workshop)
  • Speaker name(s) and short bio
  • Whether this is new or previously presented material
  • Any special requirements (network, hardware, power)

What We Do Not Accept

  • Product and sales pitches
  • Talks you cannot give without your employer's slide template
  • Content that outs or endangers real people

Speaker Notes

Accepted speakers get a free ticket. We are a non-profit community con, so we cannot cover travel or accommodation, but reach out if cost is the only thing stopping you from speaking.
